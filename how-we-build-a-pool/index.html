How a Swimming Pool Is Built, Step by Step

From the first spade in your lawn to the first swim: what happens at each stage of building a pool, with photos from our own builds.

Get a free quote

The four stages

1. Planning and digging

We agree on the size, shape and position of the pool, mark it out, and dig. Access to the back garden and where the soil goes are sorted out before we start.

2. Building the shell

The walls, floor and steps are built and plastered. This is the structure that holds the water, so it is the most important stage.

3. Coping and finish

Coping goes around the edge, then the pool gets its finish: marbelite, tiles, or marbelite with a tiled waterline.

4. Paving, filling and handover

We pave around the pool, connect the pump and filter, fill it and show you how everything works. Decking, a decked cover or an automated cover can be added now or later.

Do I need council approval for a pool?

Pools usually need building plans approved by your municipality, and must meet the local safety rules (a fence or cover). Check with your municipality before the build starts. [Henry to confirm whether he helps with plans.]

Marbelite or tiles?

Marbelite is the classic smooth plaster finish and costs less. A fully tiled pool costs more but lasts longer and looks more modern. Many pools use marbelite with a tiled waterline.

When is the best time to build?

Before summer, so the pool is ready when it gets hot. Book early: spring and early summer are the busiest months.
